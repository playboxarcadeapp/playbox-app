Playbox Arcade

Eight fun games in one app for kids and families: Brick Bash, Zoom Dash, Gem Mine, Potion Sort, Cube Escape, Galaxy Racer, Castle Crash and Anaconda. No ads, ever. No sign-ups, no internet needed to play.

Playbox Plus

Playbox is free to play every day. Playbox Plus unlocks all 500 levels in every level game, unlimited play, the Swag Shop and daily gifts. To cancel, use your Apple ID subscriptions (Settings > your name > Subscriptions) or Google Play (Profile > Payments & subscriptions). Got a new device? Open the Playbox Plus screen and tap "Restore purchases".
