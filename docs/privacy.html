Playbox Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Playbox Arcade is a collection of games made for kids and families. We built it so that it does not collect any information about the people who play it.

Information we collect

None. Playbox does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for an email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Information kept on your device

Game progress, high scores, daily streaks, the number of free plays used today, and the short player names typed into the high-score lists are saved only on your own device so the games can remember them. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Playbox shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. The games do not need an internet connection to play.

Playbox Plus subscription

Playbox is free to play each day, and an optional Playbox Plus subscription unlocks everything. Subscriptions are bought and managed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your name, email address or payment details. To check whether Playbox Plus is active, the app asks the App Store or Google Play on your device; that answer stays on the device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Apple's and Google's handling of purchase information is covered by their own privacy policies.

Grown-ups only areas

The subscription screen, and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links inside it, are behind a grown-ups-only question so children cannot open them by accident. There are no other links that take players out of the app.

Children's privacy

Because Playbox does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Purchases can only be started by a grown-up after the grown-ups-only question, and are completed in the App Store or Google Play, which ask for the account holder's confirmation.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
