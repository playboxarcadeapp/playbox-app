Playbox Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Playbox Arcade is a collection of games made for kids and families. We built it so that it does not collect any information about the people who play it.

Information we collect

None. Playbox does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for an email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Information kept on your device

Game progress, high scores, daily streaks and the short player names typed into the high-score lists are saved only on your own device so the games can remember them. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Playbox shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or third-party software development kits. It does not need an internet connection to play.

Purchases and links

Playbox has no in-app purchases and no links that take players out of the app.

Children's privacy

Because Playbox does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
