## Full description (both stores)
Playbox Arcade puts nine fun games in one app, so kids have one safe place to play.

THE GAMES
- Zoom Dash: run as a cheeky raccoon from the farm to the big city, then on to the beach, desert, jungle, snowy mountains, a neon night city and a volcano. Jump, slide, grab snacks, and see how many laps you can go.
- Galaxy Racer: steer a starfighter through glowing galaxies, dodge asteroids and race rival ships to the finish. Upgrade your ship in the Hangar (Playbox Plus) to take on tougher sectors. 500 levels.
- Castle Crash: aim a catapult at cartoon castles and knock every block off the table. 500 levels.
- Gem Mine: swap and match sparkling gems deep in the mine. Every gem has its own shape. 500 levels.
- Potion Sort: help the wizard sort magic potions before the timer runs out. 500 levels.
- Brick Bash: neon brick breaking with picture puzzles, TNT and steel bricks, and 8 themed worlds. 500 levels.
- Cube Escape: slide colored blocks out through the matching exit. 500 levels.
- Anaconda: the classic snake game in the jungle.
- Dot Friends: connect each little dot to its twin without crossing lines. No timer, so it's perfect for little kids and quiet time. 500 levels.
- My Fish Tank: a calm aquarium to look after and grow. Feed your fish, wipe the glass, collect pearls, and use the coins you earn in every game to buy new fish, decorations and bigger tanks. Visiting unlocks special surprises, and missing a day never costs anything.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
- No ads, ever
- No accounts or sign-ups
- No data collected
- Plays offline, no internet needed
- Purchases are behind a grown-ups-only question

FREE TO PLAY EVERY DAY
Every game is free to play each day: 2 new levels a day in each level game, 3 plays a day in Zoom Dash and Anaconda, plus the Daily Challenge. Replaying levels you've beaten is always free.

PLAYBOX PLUS
Unlock everything with Playbox Plus:
- All 500 levels in Castle Crash, Gem Mine, Potion Sort, Cube Escape and Galaxy Racer
- Unlimited play in every game
- The Swag Shop: outfits, ships, colors and power-ups
- The Galaxy Racer Hangar: upgrade your starfighter's engine, boosters, thrusters and shields
- Daily gifts and treasure chests
- Share with your family through Family Sharing

Playbox Plus is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year (prices may vary by country), with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID / Google Play account at confirmation of purchase, or when the free trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your account settings.

## Privacy answers
- Apple App Privacy: "Data Not Collected".
